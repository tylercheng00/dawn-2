How much thrust? 推力有多大？

A rocket pushes itself forward by throwing mass backwards fast. Thrust is the mass thrown out per second times how fast it leaves the nozzle. · 火箭靠高速向後噴出物質而前進：推力＝每秒噴出的質量 × 噴出速度。

F = ṁ · vₑ + (pₑ − pₐ) · AₑF thrust · ṁ mass flow rate · vₑ exhaust velocity · p pressures · Aₑ nozzle exit area

Can it lift off? 能否起飛？

To leave the pad, thrust must beat the rocket's weight: thrust-to-weight ratio (T/W) must be above 1. · 要離開發射台，推力必須大於火箭重量，即推重比大於 1。

Launch to space 發射上太空

Design a small single-stage sounding rocket and launch it straight up. The simulator steps through the flight every 0.05 s, including weakening gravity, thinning air and drag, and the rocket getting lighter as it burns propellant. · 設計一枚單級探空火箭並垂直發射。模擬器每 0.05 秒計算一次，包括隨高度減弱的重力、變稀薄的空氣、空氣阻力，以及燃燒推進劑後變輕的火箭。
